## Demand Validation Evidence
1. **Live Pricing Validation:** Chatbase (`chatbase.co/pricing`) charges **$150–$500/mo** for business plans plus **$99/mo** solely to remove branding and **$25/mo** per extra agent, while DIY tools leave SMB owners to configure prompts and lead capture themselves.
2. **Agency Delivery Benchmarks:** Verified market benchmarks show agencies charge **$500–$3,500 setup** + **$200–$1,500/mo** for custom AI lead-qualification and appointment concierges.
3. **Prospect Gap Verification (2026-11-26):**
   - `dciporto.pt` lacks Schema.org JSON-LD (`ld+json=0`) and relies on static forms + basic WhatsApp redirect without structured 24/7 qualification.
   - `wheelt.pt` (web design agency) has zero chat/WhatsApp conversion widget (`chat=False, wa=False`) and represents a multiplier partner channel (agencies serve dozens of SMB clients).
   - `iron.pt`, `clinicamacro.pt`, `sdadentalstudio.co.uk` use static forms or generic WhatsApp links where after-hours visitors cannot get instant service-specific answers, pricing guidance, and structured appointment pre-qualification.
4. **Why #1 Wins:**
   - **Pre-sold visual proof:** We automatically generate a **live, working, branded interactive concierge demo** populated from each prospect's real public website BEFORE sending personalized outreach.
   - **Immediate ROI math:** One captured dental implant / cosmetic case / agency project (€800–€3,000+) covers the entire setup fee and several months of retainer.
   - **Zero-cost scalable core:** Built as a lightweight static/serverless SaaS widget + prospect demo engine hosted at €0 on Firebase Hosting / GitHub Pages, upgradable to full LLM backend upon first paid invoice.
